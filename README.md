# Netflix-Content-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI-Project-
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Netflix content to uncover trends in genres, ratings, release years, and content distribution. The project helps understand viewing patterns and supports data-driven decisions in content strategy.

Netflix has a vast library of content across multiple genres, countries, and formats. However, understanding content distribution, growth trends, and audience preferences can be challenging without proper data analysis.

The objective of this project is to analyze Netflix’s content dataset to identify trends in content type, release patterns, genre popularity, and ratings. The insights derived will help in understanding how content strategy has evolved and what factors influence content availability.
The solution involves using Power BI to process and visualize Netflix data. Key steps include:

* Data cleaning and preprocessing (handling null values, formatting)
* Data modeling and relationship building
* Creating KPIs (Total Titles, Movies vs TV Shows, Release Trends)
* Designing interactive dashboards with filters and visuals

The final dashboard enables users to explore content trends and gain actionable insights.
* Total Titles on Netflix
* Number of Movies vs TV Shows
* Content Added Over Time
* Top Genres Distribution
* Ratings Distribution
## Key Insights from Dashboard

* Netflix has significantly increased content production in recent years
* Movies dominate the platform compared to TV shows
* Certain genres like Drama and Comedy are most common
* Content is heavily concentrated in specific countries
* Ratings indicate a focus on mature audience content

Overall, the dashboard shows Netflix’s strategic shift towards expanding content variety and global reach.
## Business Insights

* Netflix is focusing on content expansion to retain and attract users
* Popular genres can guide future content investments
* Regional content growth shows localization strategy
* Understanding ratings helps target specific audience segments
## Recommendations

* Invest more in high-performing genres like Drama and Comedy
* Expand content production in emerging regions
* Balance between movies and TV shows for better engagement
* Use viewer preference data for personalized recommendations
